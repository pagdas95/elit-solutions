## The challenge

Customers asked for shipment updates by email and phone. The operations team answered by copying information out of
their internal system, and documents were sent as attachments, one request at a time.

## What we built

A secure web portal where each customer signs in, sees the live status of their shipments, downloads documents and
raises requests. Status and documents sync from the operations system through an integration, so the team never
enters the same data twice.

## The result

Customers serve themselves for routine questions, and the team spends its time on exceptions instead of updates.
