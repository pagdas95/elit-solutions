## The challenge

The old site was slow, hard to update and organised around the company's internal structure rather than its
clients' questions. Enquiries arrived without the details needed to respond.

## What we built

A new site with a service structure based on what clients search for, a design system built on the brand, and an
enquiry form that collects the information the team needs to reply properly.

## The result

A site the team can update themselves, and enquiries that arrive ready to answer.
