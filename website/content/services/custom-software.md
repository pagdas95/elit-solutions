Off-the-shelf tools fit most businesses most of the time. When they stop fitting, teams end up working around them
with spreadsheets, copy-paste and manual checks. Custom software removes that friction by fitting the tool to the
process, not the other way round.

## How we approach it

We work in short iterations. You see working software early, give feedback on the real thing, and we adjust before
small decisions become expensive ones. Each release is tested and documented.

## What you get

Software that does exactly what your team needs, code that you own, and a clear plan for running and extending it.
