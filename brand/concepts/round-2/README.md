# Round 2: logo refinement on Concept A

Concept A's system (Signal) kept. Its logo is replaced by two directions based on the client's reference images, on the
client's exploratory palette. **Not final:** pick one (or ask for changes) before the brand kit is built.

| Palette | Hex | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Midnight | `#080D1B` | ground |
| Navy | `#17243B` | surfaces, cards |
| Cyan | `#36D9FF` | mark, actions (1.56:1 on Ice White: fill only on light grounds) |
| Violet | `#9174FF` | motion, glow |
| Ice White | `#F4F7FF` | type on dark |
| Layer Blue | `#5FA2FF` | computed midpoint, Layers mark and charts only |
| Deep Cyan / Blue / Violet | `#0A86B0` / `#2F6BE0` / `#6A4BEB` | the mark on light grounds (all ≥ 3:1 on Ice White) |

- **Forward** (`forward/`): an E whose arms fold into a chevron, with the violet middle bar running through the open apex.
- **Layers** (`layers/`): Phase 1's segmented E with depth: a neutral spine plus three sheared slabs, cyan → blue → violet in flat steps.
- Wordmark: **Lexend Bold**, outlined. Its slab-serifed capital I stops "ElIT" reading as "EIIT", even in one colour.
  "IT" is in Cyan and "SOLUTIONS" is outlined Lexend Light. UI and headline type: Geologica (Greek, tabular figures)
  and JetBrains Mono.

Files: `comparison/comparison.png`, `<direction>/board.png`, `website.png`, `business-card.png`, and `logo/` (outlined
SVG lockups: primary, compact, stacked and wordmark on dark, light and one-colour; symbol; gradient digital
treatment; app icons; favicons and `.ico`).
